**Title:** How to choose a wedding DJ: 8 questions to ask before you book
**Address (slug):** how-to-choose-a-wedding-dj
**Summary:** The music decides how your sangeet and reception feel. Here are eight questions to ask any wedding DJ before you pay an advance.
**Tags:** Weddings, Musicians & DJs
**Primary keyword:** wedding DJ in Ahmedabad
**Related keywords:** DJ for sangeet, Garba night DJ, live band for wedding, wedding entertainment

---

A good wedding DJ can fill a dance floor from the first song. A poor match can leave a sangeet half empty and a reception awkward. Most of that difference is decided before the event, by the questions you ask when you book.

Whether you are looking for a wedding DJ in Ahmedabad, Surat or anywhere else in Gujarat, these eight questions will help you choose.

## 1. Have you played at events like mine?

A sangeet, a Garba night, a reception and a corporate dinner need different music and a different pace. Ask what kinds of events the DJ plays most often. Ask for a recent example of an event close to yours, and how the evening went.

## 2. Can I hear or see your work?

Ask for video clips from real events, not only a studio mix. Look at how the DJ talks to the crowd, how songs flow into each other and how the floor reacts. A short clip tells you more than a long sales pitch.

## 3. How do you plan the music with us?

A good DJ asks about your families, your guests and the moments you care about. Ask how they take your must-play and do-not-play lists, and whether they plan the entry songs, the couple's dance and the Garba rounds with you.

## 4. How do you read the crowd?

No list survives a real crowd untouched. Ask what the DJ does when the floor is slow, when the elders want older songs, or when the young guests want something louder. You want someone who adjusts without making the night feel chaotic.

## 5. What equipment will you bring?

Ask about speakers, mixers, lights and backup gear. Find out what the DJ brings and what the venue is expected to provide. Ask what happens if something fails during the event. A DJ with backup equipment is one less thing for you to worry about.

## 6. How early do you arrive, and how long do you play?

Sound checks take time. Ask when the DJ will reach the venue and set up, and how long the booking covers. Be clear about the start and end times, and what happens if the event runs late.

## 7. Do you also offer a live band, a singer or an anchor?

Many weddings mix a DJ with live music, a singer or a host to keep the evening moving. If you want that, ask whether the DJ can work with other artists, or whether you should book them separately. Booking everyone for one event through a single order keeps the details in one place.

## 8. What is in the booking terms?

Before you pay an advance, get the details in writing: the date, timings, venue, what is included, the total cost, the advance and balance, and what happens if either side needs to cancel or reschedule. If anything is unclear, ask now, not on the wedding day.

## A short checklist

- Played at events like yours
- Clips from real events
- A plan for your music and your crowd
- Equipment and a backup
- Clear arrival time and playing hours
- Clear terms, in writing

# Post 2 (full draft)

**Title:** Bridal makeup artist trial: what to ask and what to bring
**Address (slug):** bridal-makeup-artist-trial-what-to-ask
**Summary:** A trial is the best way to know how your bridal look will turn out. Here is what to bring, what to ask and how to judge the result.
**Tags:** Weddings, Makeup Artists
**Primary keyword:** bridal makeup artist in Ahmedabad
**Related keywords:** bridal makeup trial, engagement makeup, party makeup, hair and makeup for events, haldi and mehndi look

---

Your bridal look will be in every photo and every video for years. A makeup trial lets you see it, and fix it, long before the wedding morning. If you are choosing a bridal makeup artist in Ahmedabad or elsewhere in Gujarat, the trial tells you more than any portfolio.

## Why a trial matters

Photos of someone else's work show the artist's style, not how it will look on your skin, in your outfit, under your wedding lights. A trial shows you three things: whether you like the finish, whether the artist listens, and whether the look lasts through the day.

## When to book it

Book the trial a few weeks before the wedding. That leaves time to change the look, the artist or the plan if something is not right. If you have more than one function, such as haldi, mehndi, sangeet and the wedding itself, ask whether the artist can plan all the looks together.

## What to bring

- **Pictures of looks you like**, and a few you do not, so the artist knows your taste.
- **Your outfit or its colours**, and your jewellery or a photo of it. The makeup should suit what you will wear.
- **Your dupatta or veil**, if it will sit close to your face or hair.
- **Your usual skincare and any allergies**, written down. Mention anything that has ever caused a reaction.
- **A photo of your hair**, and your plan for it: open, tied, with extensions or flowers.

## Questions to ask the artist

1. **Which products will you use on my skin?** Ask about the brands, and whether they suit your skin type.
2. **Do you do hair as well?** If not, ask who will, and whether they work together.
3. **How long will the look take on the day?** Work back from the muhurat to set the start time, and plan extra time for draping and jewellery.
4. **Will you come to my venue or home?** Ask what travel and early-morning arrangements are needed.
5. **Who will do the makeup on the day?** Make sure it is the person doing your trial, not an assistant.
6. **What do you carry for touch-ups?** Ask whether they stay for a few hours or only until the ceremony.
7. **Do you bring your own lighting, a chair and a mirror?** Or should the venue provide them?
8. **What are the terms?** Get the date, timings, what is included, the total cost, the advance and balance, and the cancellation and rescheduling terms in writing before you pay.

## How to judge the trial

- Look in daylight and indoors. A look that is perfect under one light can be heavy in another.
- Take photos with flash and without. Wedding photographers use both.
- Check the colour match at the jawline and neck.
- Wear it for a few hours. Does it last, or fade and crease?
- Say what you want changed. A good artist adjusts without making you feel awkward.

## Red flags

- The artist does not ask about your skin, outfit or allergies.
- The look is the same as every other bride's.
- The trial is rushed, or only a half face is done.
- The artist will not put the terms in writing.
- You feel pushed to choose something you do not like.

## A short checklist

- Trial booked a few weeks ahead
- Reference photos, outfit and jewellery brought
- Allergies and skin type told
- Products and timings asked about
- Same artist on the day
- Terms in writing
